Its own driver, written in Rust.

Mobius ships mobiusagent-driver instead of wrapping slower tools. One process talks to Android over adb and to iPhones through WebDriverAgent, for real devices, emulators and simulators.

On your phone in three steps.

Plug in a phone or boot a simulator

Android: turn on USB debugging. iPhone or simulator: build the runner once with your Apple team id.

MOBIUS_TEAM_ID=XXXXXXXXXX \ npx -y @anuraghq/mobius setup-ios

Add Mobius to your agent

Claude Code, or any MCP client with this config.

{ "mcpServers": { "mobius": { "command": "npx", "args": ["-y", "@anuraghq/mobius", "mcp"] } } }

Ask for something

The agent finds the device, opens apps and works through screens. You approve the moments that matter.

"Open Settings on my phone and turn on dark mode."

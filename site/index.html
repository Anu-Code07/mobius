Its own driver, written in Rust.

Mobius ships mobiusagent-driver instead of wrapping slower tools. One process talks to Android over adb and to iPhones through WebDriverAgent, for real devices, emulators and simulators.

Real apps, a real iPhone, one afternoon.

Everything in the film ran through Mobius on a personal iPhone 16, driven by an agent. Here is what Mobius decided at each step, straight from its signed audit log.

  1. SwiggySearched "burger", added a Crispy Veg Double Patty, opened the cart. The tap on "Pay ₹128 using PhonePe UPI" stopped for approval. Rejected, nothing paid.
  2. InstagramOpened Reels and scrolled three reels. Allowed by a policy rule: scrolling is reading. Likes, comments and sends would still ask.
  3. Apple MapsSearched Cubbon Park, pinched to zoom, saved a screenshot.
  4. MyntraFound a kurta whose card has no accessibility label: Mobius read the pixels with OCR and judged what was drawn. "Buy Now" stopped for approval.

Rules are plain JSON in MOBIUS_POLICY. They can relax a default ask, never a hard block, and every decision records the rule that made it. The run also turned up six edge cases in real apps; each is now fixed and covered by a test.

On your phone in three steps.

Plug in a phone or boot a simulator

Android: turn on USB debugging. iPhone or simulator: build the runner once with your Apple team id.

MOBIUS_TEAM_ID=XXXXXXXXXX \ npx -y @anuraghq/mobius setup-ios

Add Mobius to your agent

Claude Code, or any MCP client with this config.

{ "mcpServers": { "mobius": { "command": "npx", "args": ["-y", "@anuraghq/mobius", "mcp"] } } }

Ask for something

The agent finds the device, opens apps and works through screens. You approve the moments that matter.

"Open Settings on my phone and turn on dark mode."
